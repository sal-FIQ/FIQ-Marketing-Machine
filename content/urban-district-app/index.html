Where it stands

Urban District is a credible, full-service design-build firm in one of Canada's most competitive renovation markets. The fundamentals are strong — what's missing is a visibility & proof layer. In this market, buyers choose on reviews, proof, and local search, and that's exactly where the company is nearly invisible today.

The single highest-leverage move: build a visible proof engine — reviews, case studies, and local search presence — on top of the credibility the company already has. Almost everything else compounds from there.

The buyer journey in this market

Renovation & build-out buyers (residential tickets routinely $75K–$500K+) move through a proof-led, local-search-led path:

1 · Trigger → 2 · Discover (Google Map Pack, HomeStars, Houzz, referrals) → 3 · Shortlist on reviews + portfolio → 4 · Evaluate proof & quote → 5 · Hire the lowest-risk, most credible firm
