Where it stands

Urban District is a credible, full-service design-build firm in one of Canada's most competitive renovation markets. The fundamentals are strong — what's missing is a visibility & proof layer. In this market, buyers choose on reviews, proof, and local search — exactly where the firm is nearly invisible today.

The single highest-leverage move: build a visible proof engine — reviews, case studies, and local search presence — on top of the credibility the firm already has. Everything else compounds from there.
