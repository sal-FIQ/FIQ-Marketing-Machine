# Urban District (Tile E-commerce) — Performance Marketer Call Prep

> **Source material** for the deliverable at
> `output/urban-district-tile-ecommerce/`. This is the raw interview-prep research
> for Sal Avelar's ~15-minute call with Adam Tenzythoff, CEO of Urban District
> (the tile e-commerce venture — distinct from Urban District Developments, the
> construction firm). Captured June 9, 2026.

## Quick brief

- **What:** D2C/B2B e-commerce platform selling tile — "World's Largest Tile Selection," 10,000+ SKUs.
- **Model:** Pure-play e-commerce disrupting traditional tile sourcing (showrooms, distributors).
- **Customers:** Builders/contractors (B2B, ~60–65% of industry volume) + homeowners/designers (B2C, higher margin).
- **Key funnel mechanic:** Free sample program (up to 25/week) → **sample-to-purchase conversion is the critical metric**.
- **Competitors:** TileBar (design-focused), The Tile Shop (omnichannel). Urban District differentiates on catalog breadth + builder-centric positioning.
- **Stage:** Founded 2018 (as Urban District Developments Inc.); e-commerce platform more recent. Bootstrapped / "well capitalized." Lean team (1–10). Hiring a Performance Marketer = product/ops foundation built, now prioritizing acquisition + revenue growth.

## Adam Tenzythoff

- Founder & CEO. Former carpenter → construction/development → e-commerce. Mohawk College grad.
- Also runs Urban District Developments Inc. (construction/design-build, Oakville) and Constructive Group.
- Style: hands-on builder, "think like builders," customer obsession, data-driven, operational excellence. Respects practical, results-oriented, outcome-led thinking.

## Company values (mirror these)

1. **Customer-Centricity** — start with the customer, work backward.
2. **Operational Excellence** — deliver consistently at scale.
3. **Reinvention for Disruption** — experiment, challenge existing models.
4. **Think Like Builders** — precision, purpose, real-world execution.

## Candidate proof points (Sal)

- **$27M** net-new business at Klick (+ ~$60M marketing-sourced pipeline; ~$7.5M from events; 45% lift in outbound reply rates via AI).
- **55%** of net-new revenue from marketing at Maropost (system rebuilt from scratch; AI agents + automation; scaled without linear spend/headcount).
- **$19M** e-store revenue (+40%, +35% engagement) at Canon; referral program → **300%** increase in sign-ups + $1.2M in Q1.
- **$60M+** pipeline at NexJ → contributed to acquisition by Constellation Software.

## Throughline

Canon (modernize growth / e-commerce revenue) → Sun Life (scale into acquisition ecosystems) → NexJ (turn systems into enterprise value → acquired) → Klick ($27M net-new via AI) → Maropost (unified AI-enabled growth system, 55% of net-new revenue). Now seeking an environment to compound that impact over several years.

## Strategic questions to ask (pick 3–4)

- First 90 days: build the function from scratch, or optimize what's running?
- What drives most acquisition today, and where's the biggest untapped opportunity?
- "Aggressive demand gen" — net-new acquisition, AOV from existing, or both?
- B2B (builders/designers) vs B2C (homeowners) — which is the primary growth focus?
- First dedicated performance hire, or existing infrastructure?
- Current marketing/analytics tech stack, or is defining it part of the role?
- What does wildly successful in 6 months look like?
- How is the sample-to-purchase funnel measured today — data infra in place, or part of the build?

## Red flags to listen for

Unclear role scope ("do everything"); no budget clarity; unrealistic timelines (massive results in 30 days, no infra); one-person team forever; founder micromanagement; vague compensation; no data infrastructure; ambiguity between the two entities (which entity employs you, reporting line).

## Post-call alignment dimensions (score 1–5)

Ownership & autonomy · Resources & budget · Founder alignment · Growth potential · Compensation fit · Learning & challenge · Longevity fit · Market opportunity.
**Decision rule:** 6+ dimensions ≥4 → strong proceed; 3+ below 3 → serious concerns to resolve first.

## Cheat sheet essentials

- **30-sec positioning:** "I build growth systems that drive measurable revenue. At Maropost, marketing drove 55% of net-new revenue under a system I built from scratch. At Klick, $27M in net-new business. I'm looking for an environment where I can own demand generation end-to-end and compound that impact over several years."
- **Executive phrases:** "The way I'd think about that is…" / "I'd break that into two parts…" / "What I've learned building these systems is…" / "The real leverage point in e-commerce like this is…" / "If I were stepping into this role, I'd start with…"
- **Traps to avoid:** over-explaining career history; posing as a tile expert; badmouthing Maropost; asking about salary on this call; filling silences.
- **Maropost framing:** joined to stay long-term; delivered real results (55% of net-new revenue); operating environment limited compounding impact; now optimizing for a multi-year build.
- **Tone:** Calm. Sharp. Concise. Commercially aware. A peer conversation about mutual fit — not an audition.
